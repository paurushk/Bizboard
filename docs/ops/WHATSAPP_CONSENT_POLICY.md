# WhatsApp consent policy

Status: written 2026-10-01. Reminder and campaign sending stay off until the founder has read this page. The invoice with a payment link is the only template this policy allows first.

## Who may be messaged

Only a customer who has a WhatsApp opt-in flag on their record. A phone number on an invoice is not opt-in. Staff, suppliers, and leads are out of scope.

## What may be sent

1. An invoice message that includes the payment link for that invoice (`invoice_with_payment_link`).
2. A reminder for an unpaid invoice, only after this policy is accepted and only to the same opted-in customer.
3. No campaign or bulk send in this phase.

## How to opt out

The customer reply STOP, or a staff member clears the WhatsApp opt-in flag. Either one stops further Cloud sends. The wa.me share link remains a manual action by staff.

## Failure

If Cloud credentials are missing, or the template is not approved, the product shows the wa.me link and does not claim the message was delivered.
