# Bizboard UX Audit, 2026-09-30

Method and scope: see `docs/UX_MASTER_EXECUTION_PLAN.md`. Actions: `docs/UX_ACTION_ITEMS.md`. Data: `docs/ux/` (`L1_surface_ledger.csv`, `L2_finding_ledger.csv`, `static_scan.csv`, `crawl/`).

**Re-measured after the Wave 1 fixes (same day):** serious or critical axe findings fell from 6 routes (desktop) and 9 routes (393px) to **0 on both, across the 82 routes that rendered**. **Correction:** the other 59 of the 141 crawled routes had their module flag off and rendered the limited-access landing page, so they were never audited (accounting, GST returns, reports, CRM, insights, manufacturing, payroll, service modules and more). They are now marked `Gated-not-audited`; see `docs/UX_FEATURE_PHASES.md`. The findings below are described as found; `docs/UX_ACTION_ITEMS.md` lists what is already fixed in the working tree. Full Vitest run after the fixes: 138 files, 620 tests pass.

**How to read the confidence.** Each finding carries one of: *Measured* (axe or crawl on 141 pages, Chrome, mock mode), *Observed* (I looked at it in the browser), *Static* (code scan heuristic), *Prior-doc* (claimed by an earlier UX document, verified where stated). None of it has been validated with real users.

## 1. Executive UX audit

### Overall health

Bizboard's counter and document flows are already materially better than the older docs suggest, because most of the earlier UX plan has been built: scanner-first POS with a stated reason on a disabled tender, keyboard flow, device drafts, held-bill persistence, credit line, sticky dock, attention queue, shorter Sales menu, and a Receipts screen with void in a bottom sheet. Unit tests on the touched areas pass (65 files, 318 tests), and POS keyboard and friction e2e specs pass.

The remaining problems are of a different kind: **accessibility of the two most-used editors, cognitive load of the big forms, touch-target size on phones, mixed-language strings, and copy that speaks developer.**

### Top 10 findings

| # | Finding | Sev | Confidence | Ledger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New invoice and New purchase fail WCAG on unlabeled inputs (3 each), unnamed comboboxes (2 each) and low-contrast helper text | Critical | Measured | UX-N03, N04, N05 |
| 2 | Checkboxes in tables have no name (24 on Settings > Users, 2 on Sales history): bulk and permission controls unusable by screen reader | Critical | Measured | UX-N02 |
| 3 | Cognitive load is concentrated in five screens: Products (121 controls), New invoice (120), New purchase (102), Item dialog (101), Invoice detail (82) | High | Static + Observed | UX-N12 |
| 4 | Small touch targets: average 10 per page under 44px at phone width; invoice editors 30 or more | High | Measured | UX-N08 |
| 5 | Wide tables that scroll sideways are not keyboard-focusable on 5 screens | High | Measured | UX-N07 |
| 6 | The "nothing chosen yet" Complete hint said "customer/supplier" on both editors and offered no action (named blockers already exist once a party and line are set). Wording now fixed; the focus action remains | Medium | Observed | UX-N14 |
| 7 | About 190 hard-coded English strings bypass i18n on Products, Item dialog, Invoice detail, GST settings, Users; parity test cannot see them | Medium | Static (samples verified) | UX-N10 |
| 8 | 68 of 141 pages have no `h1` | Medium | Measured | UX-N09 |
| 9 | Copy in Collections and error cards is developer language ("not a prediction model", "Request failed with status code 500") | Medium | Observed | UX-N01, N16 |
| 10 | The POS expiry chip built earlier never rendered (API sends no expiry on Product); TypeScript build was red until fixed | Medium | Measured (tsc) | UX-025 |

### Quick wins vs. strategic bets

| Quick wins (S effort, Wave 1 to 3) | Strategic bets (L effort, needs design) |
|---|---|
| Theme contrast token, labels on checkboxes and inputs, combobox names, error copy, Collections copy, one h1 per page | Progressive disclosure for invoice, purchase and item forms; 44px touch-target program; state-wrapper and page-title patterns; real-user baselines |

### What already works (keep)

Scan-first POS with `aria-describedby` on the tender blocker; tender emphasis on the remembered method; focus stays in the scan field; Save draft and device draft restore; credit line; the Needs-attention queue; Support ID plus Retry on API errors; receipt void confirm as a bottom sheet on narrow screens; no horizontal overflow at 393px on any of 141 crawled pages; average mock-mode render 1.4s, worst 2.2s.
