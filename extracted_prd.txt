Guiding Principle
"One platform to run the entire business—from quotation to payment, purchase to inventory, accounting to GST compliance, with minimal manual effort and maximum business visibility."
This PRD is intentionally concise yet comprehensive. It is organized around business workflows rather than isolated features, making it suitable as a foundation for UI/UX design, technical architecture, database design, API contracts, and implementation planning without introducing unnecessary complexity.
I actually think you can simplify the MVP even further. Nearly 90% of MSMEs primarily use four workflows every day. Everything else (Accounting, GST returns, Manufacturing, CRM, etc.) is either derived automatically or added later.

Why this scope?
These four core flows cover the daily operations of most Indian MSMEs:
Purchase Management – Procure goods and update stock.
Sales & Invoice Generation – Create GST invoices, print on A4 or thermal printers, generate PDFs, and collect payments.
Inventory & Stock Management – Keep inventory synchronized automatically across purchases, sales, returns, and adjustments.
Basic Reports & Dashboard – Provide immediate visibility into sales, purchases, inventory, and outstanding balances.

22. Technical Principles
Modular architecture with clear domain boundaries.
REST-first APIs with consistent request/response contracts.
Stateless backend services.
Business rules centralized in the service layer.
Database transactions for all inventory and financial updates.
Inventory synchronization driven by business events (purchase, sale, return, adjustment).
Asynchronous processing for long-running tasks (PDFs, notifications, backups).
Role-based authorization enforced at both API and business-service layers.
Audit logging for all create, update, delete, and financial operations.
Configuration-driven settings (taxes, invoice numbering, templates) without code changes.
Extensible architecture that supports future modules (GST integrations, accounting, manufacturing, AI) without breaking existing APIs.

Technical Philosophy
Keep the architecture modular, event-driven, and API-first. Business operations (Purchase, Sales, Inventory) should be the source of truth, with inventory synchronization, ledgers, reports, and future accounting generated consistently from those business events rather than through duplicate data entry. This provides a maintainable foundation that can evolve into a full ERP without requiring architectural rewrites.
