GST, trial and what every plan includes

  • GST: prices are before GST. 18% GST is added to paid plans and shown on the Bizboard invoice you receive. Draft — not final
  • Trial: 14 days, no card, one trial per business. After it ends the workspace is read-only, never deleted.
  • Paying: online payment through a live gateway is not switched on yet; how you pay for a plan will be confirmed before launch.
  • Every plan: GST invoices in English or Hindi, stock and godowns, purchases, payments, and a full export of your data.
  • GST worksheets for your CA (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B): included in the trial and in every plan, Free included.
  • Not included in any plan in this release: filing on the GST portal, live e-invoice / e-way bill generation, GSTR-2B matching. See how GST worksheets work.
