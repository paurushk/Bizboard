Billing, stock and books for Indian B2B traders

Bizboard is built so the owner, the counter staff and the CA work from the same numbers. Turn a purchase bill into stock and payables in one step, make GST invoices in English or Hindi, keep track of who owes you, and hand your CA month-end GST worksheets to file from.

Who it is for

Bizboard is made first for semi-wholesale and B2B traders — roughly ₹1–5 crore turnover, one GSTIN, selling on credit to shops and businesses. It also suits counter retail shops that need a quick POS, and traders who keep stock in more than one godown.

  • Owner

    Sees sales, stock, dues and cash in one place, on a computer or a phone.

  • Counter and booking staff

    Make invoices, quotations and orders quickly, with roles that limit what each person can see and change.

  • Accountant

    Records receipts and payments, matches them to bills, and keeps the books when you switch them on.

  • Your CA

    Gets GST worksheets and reports from the same numbers your team entered — no re-typing.

What it does

  • GST invoices

    Sales invoices with CGST/SGST or IGST worked out from the place of supply, plus nil-rated and non-GST bills. PDF invoices, quotations, sales orders, delivery challans, returns and credit notes.

  • Stock and godowns

    Stock goes down when you sell and up when you buy. Godowns, transfers, batches and expiry, serial numbers, stock counts and stock value (weighted average cost).

  • Purchases

    Completing a purchase bill adds stock and what you owe the supplier in one step. Upload a bill to get a draft you check before saving. Debit notes and purchase returns.

  • Payments in and out

    Record receipts and supplier payments and allocate them to bills. Party ledgers, cheques, TDS and TCS, and reminders for overdue dues that you share by link.

  • Accounting

    Optional books per company: every entry balanced, trial balance, period close with corrections. Turn it on when you are ready.

  • Counter POS

    A quick counter screen for walk-in sales on paid plans. Stock and money update the same way as a normal invoice.

Also included: dashboard and a "needs attention" list, core reports, imports and exports, user roles, and two-step sign-in for owners and accountants.

How GST worksheets work with your CA

Bizboard prepares GST worksheets. It does not file returns on the GST portal. You or your CA file on the portal, using the worksheet.

  1. Your team makes sales, purchases, returns and credit notes in Bizboard during the month.
  2. At month end, Bizboard prepares GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B worksheets from those same documents.
  3. Your CA reviews the worksheet and reports — they can be given their own sign-in with an accountant role.
  4. Your CA files the return on the GST portal as they do today.

What is not in this release:

  • Filing on the GST portal, or GSTN JSON upload files.
  • Live e-invoice (IRN) or e-way bill generation. E-invoice is a preview only — if you must issue e-invoices, make them in your existing tool and note the IRN against the Bizboard invoice.
  • GSTR-2B / IMS matching of input tax credit.
  • More than one GSTIN per company. Composition dealers are not supported yet.

Hindi and English, computer and phone

  • Hindi and English

    Use the app in Hindi or English, and make invoices in either. Each person can choose their own language.

  • Works on a phone

    Bizboard runs in the browser and is built for a computer first, with phone layouts for checking and entering on the go. There is no app-store app in this release.

  • When the internet drops

    Invoice and POS drafts can be kept on the device while offline and sent when you are back online. These drafts are stored on the device without encryption and are wiped on sign-out, so do not rely on them on a shared device.

  • Your data

    Each business's data is kept separate from every other business. Owners can export a readable copy of everything at any time.

Questions, answered honestly

Does Bizboard file my GST returns?

No. It prepares GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B worksheets from your invoices and bills. You or your CA file on the GST portal.

Can it make e-invoices or e-way bills?

Not live in this release. E-invoice is a preview, not a filed IRN. Keep using your current tool for those and record the number in Bizboard.

Can my customers pay online through Bizboard?

Not yet. Online payment collection is only in test mode until a live payment gateway is set up. You can record payments you receive by cash, cheque, UPI or bank transfer.

Does it send WhatsApp messages for me?

Not automatically. You get a link to share — for example an invoice or a payment reminder — and send it yourself on WhatsApp.

What happens when my trial ends?

The trial lasts 14 days and needs no card. After it ends your workspace becomes read-only until you choose a plan. Your data is not deleted.

Can I move my data out?

Yes. Owners can export a readable copy of their data at any time.
